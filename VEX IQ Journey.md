## Full Volume Season (2023–2024)

Before stepping up to VEX Robotics Competition and then FRC, I competed in VEX IQ with **4613B Barker Redbacks** during the **Full Volume** season - my first taste of competitive robotics.

### Power Transfer Systems

Even at this stage I was especially interested in power transfer - getting a single motor to drive more than one mechanism. I built several pneumatic PTO (power take-off) systems on VEX IQ hardware, using pneumatic pistons to engage and disengage drive between a motor and different gear trains on demand.
