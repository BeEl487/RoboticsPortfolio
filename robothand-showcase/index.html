RobotHand

A robot hand that looks at an object, finds its outline, and works out where each finger should touch it.

What it does

One 2D image in, finger servo angles out, for the open-source Amazing Hand by Pollen Robotics.

1camera image, no depth
8 pxgrip search step
0.5friction coefficient
2servo angles per finger

How it works

  1. DetectYOLOE finds the object's box; labels ignored
  2. OutlineGrabCut or edges inside the box, holes kept
  3. ScoreGrips tried along the outline, ranked by force balance
  4. SolveInverse kinematics gives each finger's servo angles
  5. GraspSpace saves it, holds 1 s, then closes the fingers

Simulation uses Pollen's hand model only, so finger geometry is not measured on a real hand. Results come from small samples. Full numbers and failures are in the decision logs in the repository.
Built with Python, OpenCV, YOLOE and MuJoCo. Hand design by Pollen Robotics (code Apache 2.0, mechanical design CC BY 4.0).
