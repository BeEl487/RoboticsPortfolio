How I work

  1. Identify. Turn the problem into numbers: what it must do, how fast, how heavy.
  2. Design. Sketch options, score them in a decision matrix, then model the winner in CAD.
  3. Build and test. Make it, measure it, and write down what failed and why.
  4. Iterate. Each failure becomes the next requirement, until the numbers are met.
